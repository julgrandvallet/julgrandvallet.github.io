## Open teaching material

**[Learning RNA-seq](https://github.com/julgrandvallet/learning-rnaseq)**
A concept-first curriculum taking someone with a quantitative background and no molecular
biology from what a transcriptome is to interpreting differential expression. Slide decks,
a FASTQ-to-counts prequel, and a sandbox for p-values and multiple-testing correction.
Built on the public `airway` dataset. Teaching material CC BY 4.0, so it can be reused and
adapted.
